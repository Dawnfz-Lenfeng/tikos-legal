# Tikos Privacy Policy

Updated: October 5, 2026

Tikos is provided by Beijing Boya Guanzhi Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains which information stays on your device and which information is processed for accounts, AI, purchases, and synchronization.

## 1. Local learning records

Question banks, questions, answers, mistakes, favorites, notes, and imported study materials are stored in your device's database or application files. Manual authoring, practice, review, and notes do not require a Tikos account. Signing out does not delete these records.

Content leaves the device when you choose a feature that requires it, as described below. Exporting or sharing sends data to the recipient you select; check the recipient and scope before sharing.

## 2. Accounts and sign-in

We process your mainland China phone number, verification records, account identifier, and session to authenticate you, provide account services, and prevent abuse. Tencent Cloud SMS delivers verification messages. Codes are stored as digests with an expiry, and the application stores its session in system secure storage.

We also process a random installation identifier, platform, application version, and language for installation registration and service operations. This identifier is not a hardware fingerprint. Tikos does not use advertising identifiers, sell personal information, or track you across applications for advertising.

## 3. AI and third-party processing

When you choose AI import, generation, grading, or tutoring, the material excerpts, questions, answers, images, or conversation content needed for the task are sent through Tikos to the AI provider. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information, including identity documents, financial accounts, and medical information. Obtain appropriate authorization before submitting another person's information.

Tikos does not persist task input or output bodies, study materials, or your local database in its business database or logs. Content is processed in memory during the task. We retain content-free task status, timing, usage, charges, and error categories for billing, recovery, and abuse prevention. Third-party AI providers process requests under their own policies.

The application currently supports DeepSeek, including a user-supplied DeepSeek API key. A key you choose to save is encrypted on Tikos servers and used to call the selected service on your behalf. The application displays only a masked hint, and you can remove the key in AI service settings. It is not used for advertising or sold. Read the [DeepSeek Privacy Policy](https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html).

## 4. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes iPhone and iPad payments; Tikos does not receive your full payment-card details. We process transaction identifiers, products, purchase or subscription status, verification receipts, the associated Tikos account, and AI-point ledger records to verify purchases, grant benefits, prevent duplicate claims, and handle refunds or revocations.

Apple's rules govern subscription management, cancellation, and refunds. Signing out or deleting a Tikos account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Manage future renewals in Apple's subscription settings.

## 5. iCloud and permissions

Optional iCloud sync uses your device's Apple ID and Apple's iCloud service. When enabled, learning records and assets synchronize between your devices through iCloud; this does not create a learning database on Tikos account servers.

You initiate file selection, image selection, and handwriting input. Tikos accesses the material needed for the selected feature and does not continuously collect contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, or your background photo library. Manage applicable permissions in system settings.

## 6. Retention, security, and use

Tikos account and service databases are hosted in mainland China. Network requests use HTTPS, user-supplied AI keys are encrypted at rest, and access is limited by responsibility. Account, transaction, and operational records are retained within the scope needed to provide services, handle transactions, maintain security, and fulfill legal obligations. Apple, Tencent Cloud SMS, and AI providers also process relevant data under their own policies; their processing locations and retention rules may differ from Tikos databases.

The current client does not send product-behavior analytics events. If optional analytics are added, we will explain their data and provide appropriate choices. Authentication, purchase verification, and AI billing are necessary operational records rather than optional analytics.

## 7. Choices and rights

You can use local features without signing in, stop AI operations to stop new content submissions, disable iCloud sync, export or delete local learning materials, remove a user-supplied API key in AI settings, or sign out.

Contact us below to request access, correction, or deletion of server-side personal information, withdraw relevant consent, or raise a privacy complaint. We verify the requester's identity and handle requests under applicable law. Legally required transaction or security records may remain restricted to their required purposes rather than being removed immediately. Manage device-local and iCloud learning records separately through their respective features.

## 8. Minors

Minors should use features involving personal information, purchases, or AI content submissions with their guardian's guidance and consent. Do not submit unrelated personal information about minors in study materials. Guardians can contact us about inappropriate processing.

Updates are published here. Material changes to personal-information purposes, methods, or categories will receive appropriate notice and consent where required.
